Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Chaos, its route, and displacement sensitivity in the
quasi-steady falling-plate model," for publication in [Venue].

The paper studies whether, where, and by what route the two-dimensional quasi-steady falling-plate
model of Andersen, Pesavento & Wang (2005) becomes chaotic as the dimensionless moment of inertia
is varied, and how sensitive the horizontal landing position is to release conditions. Using
diagnostics that I first verified on the logistic map and the Lorenz system — a variational Lyapunov
solver, Benettin's method, and the Gottwald–Melbourne 0–1 test — I confirm a chaotic band, show that
the route to chaos is *not* a Feigenbaum period-doubling cascade, establish a quantitative link
between the Lyapunov exponent and the exponential growth of landing-position sensitivity, and find no
hysteresis or non-trivial multistability. I also report a period discrepancy with the source paper at
one parameter value. All results are fully reproducible from an accompanying open-source codebase and
a single-command pipeline.
